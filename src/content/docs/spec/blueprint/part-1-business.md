## Overview

Zarnik Sightline is a hotel audit service from Kochi, led by Azhar Umar and Vijayalakshmi PV. Hotels get on-site and remote audits, a scored report, and a dashboard to track every issue.

- Sold as a service, not as software
- The Sightline app is how audits are captured, reported and followed up
- Not connected to Zarnik supplies, now or later

## Audit catalogue

Sightline offers 36 audits in 8 groups. Every audit is built from an audit template in the app.

**Department audits**

- **Housekeeping:** rooms after cleaning, public areas, linen, chemicals, equipment, records, SOP follow-through
- **Front Office:** arrival, check-in, check-out, billing accuracy, guest information, records
- **F&B Service:** restaurant, bar, room service, table setup, service speed, menu accuracy
- **Laundry & Linen:** laundry process, linen par stock, linen loss, stain and damage handling
- **Security:** access control, CCTV, key control, guard rounds, incident records
- **Spa & Recreation:** pool, gym, spa rooms, hygiene, safety, records

**Operations audits**

- **Ops & Quality:** every department in one audit
- **Brand Standards:** rooms, public areas and service against the hotel's brand standards
- **Mystery Guest:** a normal stay, booking to checkout, reported as a guest
- **Food Safety & Hygiene:** storage, temperature logs, hygiene, pest control, FSSAI records
- **Fire & Life Safety:** equipment, alarms, exits, drills, NOC and records
- **Inventory & Stores:** store access, stock accuracy, expiry, stock issued to departments, par levels
- **HR, Licences & Statutory:** PF, ESI, contracts, attendance, wages, licences on display

**Financial controls audits**

- **Financial & Accounting Practices:** cash, petty cash, bank and ledger reconciliation, GST, TDS, approvals, month-end closing
- **Procurement:** vendor list, quotes, PO approval, receiving checks, invoice matching, contracts
- **Cost Control:** food and beverage cost, wastage, recipe costing, labour cost, energy, linen and amenity use per occupied room
- **Revenue Integrity:** rate overrides, room status match, rate parity, complimentary rooms, no-show and cancellation charges, cashier floats

**Engineering & civil audits**

- **MEP & Engineering Systems:** electrical, HVAC, plumbing, DG, lifts, water treatment, STP, preventive maintenance
- **Building & Civil Condition:** roof, waterproofing, seepage, façade, visible structural defects, drainage
- **Energy & Utilities:** meters, use per occupied room, savings list
- **Engineering Complete:** all three engineering and civil audits together

**Digital audits**

- **Website:** content, speed, mobile view, booking path
- **Google & Online Presence:** Google Business Profile, maps, listings, social profiles
- **OTA Listing & Rate Parity:** listing content, photos, rate parity, ranking signals
- **Reviews & Reputation:** patterns in reviews across Google, OTAs and TripAdvisor, response quality
- **Direct Booking:** booking engine, payment flow, direct rate offer
- **IT & Guest Data Security:** PMS access rights, passwords, backups, guest data handling
- **Digital Complete:** Website, Online Presence, OTA, Reviews and Direct Booking together

**People audit**

- **Leadership Capability Review:** structured interviews and on-floor review of the GM and every department head

**Buyer and investor audits**

- **Pre-Purchase Inspection:** building condition, rooms, public areas, F&B, and 12 months of public reviews. No books. Run with the owner's permission, or as a guest
- **Pre-Purchase Assessment:** inspection plus operations and a revenue summary
- **Due Diligence:** the full picture before a purchase, lease or takeover

**Projects**

- **Annual 360 Health Check:** a yearly sample of every audit area
- **Pre-Opening Readiness:** setup, staffing, SOPs, licences and stock before opening
- **Floor Plan Audit:** room layouts, guest flow, staff flow, back-of-house, space use
- **PIP Audit:** gap list against the target standard, priority order, estimated cost per item

**Rules for sensitive audits**

- **Leadership Capability Review:** leaders are told they are being reviewed; Sightline assesses capability, the owner makes any hiring decision; results visible only to the owner or Group Head
- **Engineering & civil:** Sightline checks condition and maintenance practice; structural and electrical safety certificates still come from licensed engineers
- **Pre-Purchase Inspection as a guest:** no entry to restricted areas

## Pricing rules

Every price is days × day rate. Prices are internal and shared only on the pricing call, never on the website.

**Day rates**

- **Operations day: ₹12,000.** Department and operations audits
- **Specialist day: ₹16,000.** Financial, engineering, people, buyer and investor audits, and projects
- **Digital day: ₹7,500.** Digital audits, done remotely
- **Annual 360 Health Check: ₹14,000 a day,** a blend of operations and specialist work

**Why these rates**

- Two auditors bill about 16 audit days a month each
- 16 days × ₹12,000 = ₹1,92,000 a month per auditor from operations work alone
- Specialist work needs accounting or engineering judgement and carries more risk if something is missed
- Digital audits need no travel and open the door to on-site work

**Revenue check**

- A plan should stay under 1% of the hotel's room revenue
- Example small hotel: 30 rooms, ₹3,500 rate, 65% occupancy = about ₹20 lakh room revenue a month
- Example medium hotel: 70 rooms, ₹5,000 rate, 65% occupancy = about ₹68 lakh a month

## Plans

Quarterly is the default for most hotels. Annual plans run 12 months, billed quarterly in advance, at 10% off single-audit prices.

**Sightline Care**

- Annual 360 Health Check, then an Ops & Quality audit in each of the next three quarters
- Small ₹32,000 · Medium ₹35,000 · Large ₹49,500 a quarter

**Sightline Controls**

- One specialist audit each quarter: Financial → Procurement → Cost Control → Revenue Integrity
- Small ₹29,000 · Medium ₹36,000 · Large ₹43,000 a quarter

**Sightline Complete**

- Sightline Care + Sightline Controls + yearly Engineering Complete + yearly Digital Complete
- 15% off single-audit prices
- Small ₹81,000 · Medium ₹90,500 · Large ₹1,18,000 a quarter
- Best fit for medium and large hotels; for a small hotel it goes past 1% of room revenue

**Sightline Recovery**

- Ops & Quality audit every month for 6 months, then moves to Sightline Care
- For hotels just opened, with a new GM, or with falling reviews
- 10% off single-audit prices
- Small ₹21,600 · Medium ₹21,600 · Large ₹32,400 a month

## Lead flow

There is one lead flow: every page leads to the Request a call form.

Request a call form → Pricing call → Discovery call → Scope and contract → Onboarding → First audit

**Tools**

- **Form:** Web3Forms, sending to audits@zarnik.com, with a hidden field for the page the visitor came from
- **Lead list:** one Google Sheet
- **Discovery call booking:** Google Calendar appointment schedule
- Lead tracking stays out of the Sightline app

## Pricing call and discovery call

1. The hotel fills the Request a call form: name, hotel, rooms, location, what they need
2. Azhar or Vijayalakshmi calls and shares pricing for what they asked about
3. Price works for them → the discovery call booking link is sent
4. Price does not work → the lead is closed politely, nothing to cancel
5. On the discovery call: the hotel, its SOPs, the problem, the right audit or plan
6. Scope and contract sent from Zarnik Enterprise Services
7. Advance received → hotel created in the app → invites sent

The pricing call also checks fit: Sightline takes hotels whose owner is ready to act on findings.

## Sales and marketing

**Where demand comes from**

- **Founding clients:** the first three hotels, chosen from hotels already advised; their score changes become the first proof
- **Workshops:** a half-day "How to audit your own hotel" session alongside the existing owner workshops, ending with the next intake date
- **LinkedIn:** Azhar and Vijayalakshmi each post twice a week; real findings from audits, anonymised photo, what was wrong, what it cost, how it was fixed
- **Insights on the website:** the same findings, written up for search and AI tools
- **Annual Kerala hotel standards report:** after 20+ audits, anonymised patterns published for press, LinkedIn and AI tools

**What not to do**

- No paid ads
- No discounts
- No fake scarcity; Kerala's hotel world is small and owners talk

**First 90 days**

1. **Month 1:** audit three founding clients, record scores
2. **Month 2:** start LinkedIn posts, run one workshop, collect waitlist names
3. **Month 3:** follow-up audits show score changes; open the first public intake
